About IPO Radar

What it is

A listing-day decision aid for Indian IPOs, mainboard and SME. For every issue in the pipeline it estimates the chance of closing above the issue price, the likely gain range, your odds in the retail allotment lottery, and what one application is worth after charges and tax, then says in plain words whether it is an apply, a coin flip, or a gamble.

It exists because the usual way of chasing listing gains, trusting the grey-market premium and hoping, loses money quietly. Here the GMP is one input among several, every probability is either calibrated on past listings or labelled as a prior, and the allotment lottery is counted rather than wished away.

Who makes it

IPO Radar is a Layerbit tool, built by the same small team behind the Layerbit developer utilities and LayerLink. It is free, carries no account and no tracking, and keeps your planner inputs in your own browser only.

It is not investment advice. A 75% chance of a gain is a 25% chance of a loss, and no pre-listing number knows what the market does on listing morning. Apply only with money that can stay stuck if a listing goes wrong.
