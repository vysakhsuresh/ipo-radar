New here? Three things to know

  1. Start with the verdict. Apply, Lean apply, Coin flip, Avoid or Too early. It already weighs everything else.
  2. “70% chance” means 7 in 10. Of ten IPOs that looked like this one, about seven ended their first day above the issue price. It is not the gain you will make.
  3. Most applicants get nothing. In a popular IPO shares go by lottery. “Worth per application” counts that, so it is the number to compare.

About IPO Radar

What it is

A listing-day decision aid for Indian IPOs, mainboard and SME, made for anyone, not only for people who already know the jargon. For every current issue it estimates the chance of ending listing day above the issue price, the likely gain, your odds in the share lottery, and what one application is worth after charges and tax, then says in plain words whether it is an apply, a coin flip, or a gamble.

It exists because the usual way of chasing listing gains, trusting the unofficial premium and hoping, loses money quietly. Here that premium is one input among several, every chance is either checked against past listings or labelled as a starting estimate, and the lottery is counted rather than wished away.

Who makes it

IPO Radar is a Layerbit tool, built by the same small team behind the Layerbit developer utilities and LayerLink. It is free, carries no account and no tracking, and keeps your planner inputs in your own browser only.

It is not investment advice. A 75% chance of a gain is a 25% chance of a loss, and no pre-listing number knows what the market does on listing morning. Apply only with money that can stay stuck if a listing goes wrong.
